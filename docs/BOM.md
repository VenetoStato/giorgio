## Cost-down options (owner: "the whole thing must cost little"; safety functions stay PL d)

The **standard** configuration above already uses the cheapest parts that pass every check in `CHECKS_*.md`:

- Jetson Orin NX 16 GB (100 TOPS class) instead of AGX Orin 64 GB: it runs Gemini 336L depth + 2 fisheye + 2 wrist cams, YOLO-class detection + grasp pose (TensorRT INT8), one ACT/diffusion policy at 10-30 Hz and ASR/TTS; the LLM agent runs in the cloud. Saves ~EUR 2,250 and ~1 kg; 12 V rail drops to a DDR-60L-12 (EUR 30).
- CAN: one gs_usb CAN-FD adapter (EUR 40) next to the adapter in the OpenArm kit, instead of 2 x PEAK PCAN-USB FD (EUR 520).
- PNOZ status to the Jetson via 2 safe semiconductor outputs + optocouplers to GPIO (already in the BOM) instead of the PNOZ m ES ETH Modbus module (EUR 400).
- Kept because nothing cheaper passes: Siemens 3RT2036 (S2) contactors (45 A worst-case break at 54.75 V; S0 only ~35 A), Mean Well DDR-480C-24 (only DIN unit with 720 W for 5 s at 48 V in), class T main fuse (2.55 kA Isc), 15s 30 Ah pack (C-rate and 6 h barista).
