# AMR rev B: verification of purchased components against public documentation (2026-10-05)

Owner requirement: the design must be about 99 % certain from published information, with nothing that depends on asking a supplier.
This file takes every open supplier question (`electrical/ELECTRICAL.md` §14, `README.md` "Before pre-production", `ce/GAPS.md` G2/G3/G8)
and every ASSUMED input about a purchased component (`CALC.md`, `electrical/netlist_amr.yaml`), and tries to close it from official
manufacturer documents.

**Status rules**
- **CLOSED:** a verbatim quote from an official manufacturer document answers the question.
- **REPLACE:** the document does not answer the question, so a specific alternative is proposed whose datasheet does.
- **RESIDUAL:** the item cannot be documented publicly at all (price, physical value to be measured, no published claim). The table says why it does not affect design safety.

PDFs are saved in `../docs/fonti/` (34 MB). Large manuals are kept as page extracts and cited by URL.
Page numbers are PDF pages unless the document prints its own page numbers (ez-Wheel "Page x on 132", Pilz, SICK).

This file only reports. **No design file was changed:** `amr_cad.py`, `amr_params.py`, `electrical/*` and the BOM are untouched. The
required changes are listed at the end.

**What the 12 RESIDUAL items are:**
- **2 price rows.**
- **4 physical values to measure in the type tests:** SWD brake torque, drive reaction time, regen chopper, peak-torque duration.
- **3 battery data with no published claim:** prospective short-circuit current, vibration rating, BMS PL.
- **The PNOZ timer element**, which can be checked in the free Configurator.
- **Arm data** (Kassow, OpenArm), which is outside the base.

None of them needs a supplier answer for the base to be safe, provided the design changes at the end are applied.

**Findings the earlier rev B documents had wrong:**
- The DDR-480 and DRDN40 must not be mounted lying flat.
- The DDR remote input is the opposite logic of what was assumed.
- The LYNK II "alarm NC = OK" wiring is not fail-safe.
- The SWD has no safety-rated SS1, and its default "brake" is a motor phase short.
- The PNOZ logic time is 33–54 ms, not 10 ms.
- No catalogue D100 spring castor has a spring soft enough for the 31 N/mm window.
- RoboPad has no wireless CAN and requires ≤ 60 V under fault conditions.
- The PNOZmulti Configurator is free.
- 3RT2026 with 2 poles in series is rated 35 A DC-1 at 60 V. This is good news: the "thin" margin is resolved.

## Design changes required to reach full certainty

Changes that touch safety come first.

1. **Relay interface for SWD and scanner inputs (E6, S4, P6, P14).** Add 1–2 × Pilz **PNOZ m EF 4DI4DOR (772143)**.
   - Its positive-guided relay contacts drive SWD STO1/STO2 and INSafe, and the nanoScan3 control inputs, so no test pulses reach inputs whose tolerance is not published.
   - Set the scanner input delay to the "relay" class.
   - Fit a ~2.2 kΩ bleed resistor per contact for the 10 mA relay minimum load.
   - Cost +€380 to +€760 net; +22.5 to 45 mm on the right upper rail (≤ 6 modules OK).
   - Recompute the I/O map.
2. **Field lengths (P8, S3, E9).**
   - CALC t_logic 10 ms → **54 ms** (relay path).
   - Keep t_drive as a value to measure (no published SWD reaction time), with margin.
   - Fields grow by ~66 mm at 1.5 m/s.
3. **SS1-t timing (E4, E3).** The SWD has no safety-rated SS1, and its default SBC is a motor-phase short, not a friction brake; the parking-brake torque is unpublished.
   - Either set the PNOZ STO delay ≥ 1.1 s (it covers the 1.5 m/s → 0 ramp at 1.5 m/s²), or size the fields for STO arriving mid-ramp.
   - Make the cat-0 stopping distance and the 6 % slope holding type-test items (ISO 3691-4 brake test).
4. **T24 must sink regen (E1).** The DSR 50/5 clamp at 27 V is mandatory (FW ≥ 1.1.4 disables phase-short braking if the source cannot accept current). Keep 27 V < 28.8 V (DDR OVP) < 32 V (SWD alert).
5. **K0 chain (D4, D10, M2).**
   - The LYNK II relay must be a NO contact programmed "energised = OK" (fail-safe), with coil freewheel diodes.
   - Add a 48 V application low-voltage cut-off.
   - The DDR remote must be driven from a K0 **NC** aux contact (open = ON).
6. **Converter mounting (M1, M7).**
   - All DDR-xxx and DRDN40 must be mounted vertically, input terminals down, with 40 mm free above and 20 mm below. This applies to E50/E52 in the centre bay (no lying on P10) and to every side-bay rail.
   - Fans are required for S24 (DDR-240 maximum ambient 50 °C).
7. **Dock (M12, M13, R1, R2, R5, R6).**
   - Add a 59 V overvoltage relay that opens the dock contactor.
   - Choose NPB-750-48 (Class B, slower charging) or an EMC test of NPB-1700.
   - Set the NPB DIP "flooded" (56.8/53.6 V) instead of reprogramming.
   - RoboPad: part number RPCOL90-100, 60 A continuous, no wireless CAN (handshake over Wi-Fi/Ethernet), cables ≤ 2 m.
8. **Castors (CA1).** Replace "spring castor D100" with Blickle L-ALST 100K + our own guided slide + Gutekunst D-350 spring (28.4 N/mm). CAD rework of the castor towers (+30–40 mm, or recessed slide).
9. **PSEN cs3.1 (P16).** Wire the two sensors to separate input pairs (no series connection).
10. **E-stops (EA1).** Siemens 3SU1 E-stop with 3SU1400-1AA10-1CA0 NC blocks (positive opening documented), unless the Eaton M22-K02 datasheet becomes retrievable.
11. **K4 coil (FI1, P7).** 92 mA > 75 mA PNOZ auxiliary output: add an interposing relay (e.g. Phoenix PLC-RSC 6.2 mm) or use an SC output.
12. **Order codes and data corrections.**
    - SW80B: continuous-rated 24 V coil, A suffix and B suffix. ED250B-L. F0 = 3NA3830 NH000.
    - System plug NANSX-AAACZZZZ1 (2105107).
    - Pack ratings: 15 A continuous per pack, 116 A is a 1-hour rating, internal fuse 60 A, BMS main relay (not FETs). Keep ≥ 50 mm free above each lying pack. H01 = H02.
    - DDR-480 efficiency 0.92. DDR-60L-5 is 52.5 mm wide. LYNK II gateway is 120 × 135 × 44 mm.
    - Scanner power 15.9 W at maximum output load.
    - K1/K2 3RT2026: 35 A DC-1 with 2 poles in series is now SOURCED (no fallback needed). Route their coil wires in separate multicore cables (P4).
13. **Downloads with no supplier contact:**
    - the SWD 125 STEP file (free ez-Wheel login);
    - the PNOZmulti Configurator (free) to confirm the timer element (P15) and the 2 + 2 split of the configurable outputs (P7).
