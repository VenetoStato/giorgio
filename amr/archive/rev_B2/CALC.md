Generated by `amr/amr_calc.py` from `amr_params.py`, `out/parts.json`, `out/integration.json` and the STL files (`out/stl`, `../cad/out/stl`). Quasi-static hand calculations for design review; they do not replace the supplier confirmations listed at the end, the SISTEMA/Safety Designer calculations or the type tests. Every input is tagged (table at the end). Frame: floor, base centre, x forward, y left, z up.

**Rev B (owner decision 2026-10-05): no waist yaw joint.** The 10 mm 6082 deck (z 343..353) is the fixed superstructure flange at the old adapter height; the superstructure is fixed at yaw 0. Consequences for the checks: (a) tipping is evaluated at yaw 0 only, but because the torso can no longer turn the arms toward the load, the worst arm poses are checked **forward, to the left, to the right and to the rear**; (b) all yaw motion is the whole robot rotating in place, so the rotate-in-place field and yaw-rate limit use the swept radius of the whole robot (base + superstructure overhang); (c) the waist actuator, crossed-roller bearing, coupling and waist field checks of rev A are removed; (d) the Jetson and the arm DC-DCs sit in the base centre bay. Section numbers changed from rev A (old §5 waist removed).

## 3. Load split with sprung castors

Model: two rigid drive wheels on one axle line (x = 0) fix the body height; the body can only pitch about that line. Each castor force F = F_inst + k·(x·θ), F_inst = installed spring force (preload at installed height). Consequences: (a) the castor load total is set by the springs, not by the CoG, so the **drive-wheel share is lowest for the lightest robot (empty)**; (b) longitudinal pitching moments go to the castors (drive load unchanged while all 4 castors touch); (c) roll cannot happen without lifting a drive wheel, so **lateral moments go entirely to the drive wheels**.

- Empty robot 158.4 kg, fully loaded 166.5 kg.
- Drive wheels ≥ 35 % each on flat floor (empty robot) ⇒ F_inst ≤ 117 N per castor; target 40 % each (amr_params SUSP) ⇒ **F_inst = 78 N per castor** (castors 20 % of the empty weight).
- Floor unevenness 5 mm (ASSUMED) under the castors must not push the drive share below 35 %: k ≤ 31.1 N/mm.
- Body pitch ≤ 1° (ASSUMED, scan-plane tilt) at ±1.5 m/s², all poses: k ≥ 48.4 N/mm.
- **Spring window EMPTY: k = 48.4 .. 31.1 N/mm; chosen k = 31.1 N/mm**, spring travel 15 mm ASSUMED, installed at mid travel, hard stop at the end. With the window empty the 35 % rule is kept (k = k_max) and the pitch at ±1.5 m/s² is 1.54° (> 1° target): accept ~1.5° (scan plane drops 80 mm at 3 m - check the field against floor detection) or require a flatter floor (≤ 4 mm) / reduce F_inst.

## 2b. Emergency stop deceleration

**Rev B2 stop concept: SS1-t** (the SWD has no safety-rated SS1; its default SBC on STO is the internal motor-phase short, not a friction brake - SWD manual v2.0.2 p.24/p.106, VERIFICATION E3/E4). On a stop request the PNOZ (t = 0) requests SLS[1] 0.3 m/s and the SWD ramps at 1.5 m/s² (non-safe quick-stop ramp); the PNOZ removes STO at t = 1.2 s, after the ramp has ended at every speed (§7, SS1-t timing). If the ramp fails, the SWD SLS monitoring (t_SLS 1 s, PL d) or the PNOZ timer (1.2 s, PL e) end it with STO + phase-short braking. Cat 0 (STO with motion) therefore happens only on power loss or a ramp fault. Cat-0 decel (phase-short brake, then parking brake) is limited by tyre grip of the drive wheels only (castors free): a_cat0 ≤ μ·N_drive/m (highest for the heaviest robot). Tip limit = forward direction, worst arm pose (fwd). Braking while driving backward loads the rear: compare with the 'back' column of §2 (rear pose).

| stop | decel m/s² | a_tip fwd worst | SF fwd | a_tip back worst (reversing) | SF back | status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| SS1 ramp (configured) | 1.50 | 5.44 | 3.63 | 4.78 | 3.18 | PASS |
| cat 0 (STO + brake), grip-limited μ·N_drive/m (heaviest) | 3.97 | 5.44 | 1.37 | 4.78 | 1.20 | WARN |

The SWD parking-brake torque and the phase-short braking decel are **not published** (VERIFICATION E3, RESIDUAL): the design does not use them. The fields (§7) are sized on the SS1-t ramp, which ends before STO. Two **type tests** replace the missing data (ISO 3691-4 brake test, `ce/TEST_PLAN.md`): TP-03a cat-0 stopping distance at each speed band (power cut and STO with the ramp disabled), and TP-03b holding on a 6 % slope, loaded robot (166 kg): 3.06 Nm per wheel = 0.76 Nm at each motor (4:1), no creep > 10 mm in 10 min with power off. If the measured cat-0 decel is below the grip limit, the cat-0 tip margin is better than shown.

## 7. Protective field lengths (nanoScan3)

S = v·(t_scan 70 ms SOURCED + t_logic 54 ms SOURCED (PNOZ through the EF 4DI4DOR relays) + t_drive 40 ms ASSUMED, measure) + 1.1·v²/(2·1.5) + Z (65 mm supplement SOURCED + 0 mm reflector: valid only while no retroreflector is within 6 m of the scan plane, else ZR = 350 mm, SICK OI p.27). - **OpenArm (R&D):** no brakes, no STO. Any cat-0 stop (E-stop, protective field, power loss) cuts arm power: the arms fall and the object is dropped (and the falling arm is itself a hazard). Carry mode with OpenArm is acceptable only in R&D, fenced/attended, not in public. Keep the CONTEXT rule: objects carried in the tray while driving.
- **Certified arms (Kassow/UR):** brakes + safe stop 1/2 + safe standstill monitoring. Carry mode becomes **certifiable only with certified standstill monitoring of the arm joints (SOS / safe standstill, PL d) while the base moves**, with the base stop category SS1 and the arm in SOS, and a risk assessment of the dropped object (EN ISO 10218-2 application, EN ISO 3691-4).

## Required design changes / open items

Not applied to `amr_cad.py` / `amr_params.py` (calc stream only): to be decided and implemented in the CAD stream.

1. Castor springs / roll: castor spring window (35 % drive share vs ≤1° pitch) = k 48.4..31.1 N/mm, F_inst 78 N -> see §3.
2. Cat-0 stop decel (grip-limited) is close to the tip limit in the worst arm pose: SS1-t (STO delay 1.2 s, ramp ends before STO) is used for every stop except power loss / ramp fault; cat-0 stopping distance and 6 % slope holding are type tests (TP-03a/b); arms not extended while driving.

Either trim P21/P24/S08 so that the coffee module stays inside y ≥ -280 mm, or keep it and add the overhang to every lateral protective field (done in §7) and to the rotate-in-place circle.

11. amr_params.SUSP quotes the rev A spring limit (k ≤ 30.7 N/mm); rev B (lighter, empty robot 158.4 kg) gives F_inst 78 N per castor (40 % drive share each, empty) and k ≤ 31.1 N/mm (35 % with 5 mm floor unevenness): update the comment and the castor purchase spec.
