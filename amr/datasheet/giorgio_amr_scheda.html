Giorgio AMR / autonomous base for a bimanual service robot

Differential-drive base designed for Giorgio. It turns on the spot, carries and powers the whole robot, and docks to charge by itself. Certified safety modules plus laser-cut, bent and bolted parts. The 15 mm top deck is the torso flange, so the robot above it is unchanged.

StatusWhat is left before pre-production

  1. Every purchased-part value used in the design is taken from an official manual or datasheet (93 items checked, sources saved). Nothing depends on a supplier answer.
  2. Price quotes: castors (Blickle) and linear guides (HIWIN), plus the estimated fabricated parts.
  3. Type tests on the prototype, as the standards require: stopping distances and times, field validation with the test objects, scan-plane height, stability and slope holding, threshold climbing, castor preload, EMC and radio.
  4. Arms: OpenArm has no brakes, and Machinery Regulation Annex III 1.2.6 (d) does not allow parts that fall on power loss. Add brakes or spring balancing on the shoulder and elbow, or choose certified DC arms.
  5. Vendor STEP files in place of the datasheet envelopes, then a final interference check.
